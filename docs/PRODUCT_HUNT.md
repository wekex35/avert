## Description

```
Avert is a Mac menu bar app that soft-blurs your screen when you look away — so people beside you see less of what you’re working on.

It uses AirPods head motion (not a camera). Look left or right and that side veils; look back to center and it clears. Panic cover anytime with ⌘⇧B.

Same sensors power guided neck exercises: look down/up, tilt, and turn, with on-screen coaching and an optional voice count. Stillness reminders nudge you when you’ve been glued to the screen.

• AirPods head tracking — no camera
• Look-away privacy blur + panic cover
• Guided neck exercise set + reminders
• Everything on-device — no account, no uploads

Requires macOS 14+ and AirPods (or compatible Beats) with head motion.

Download (Apple-notarized): https://github.com/wekex35/avert/releases/download/v1.0.0-notarized/Avert-1.0.0-macos-notarized.dmg
Site: https://wekex35.github.io/avert/
```
