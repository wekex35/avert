Screen privacy

Look away. That side soft-blurs.

In a café or open office, Avert veils the side of the screen you’re exposing. Look back to center and it clears. Panic cover anytime with ⌘⇧B.

  • AirPods head tracking — no camera
  • Optional look-down dim and look-up cover
  • Everything stays on your Mac

Neck care

Guided stretches that follow your head.

Same AirPods sensors power a short exercise set — look down/up, tilt, turn — with on-screen coaching and an optional voice count.

  • Stillness and posture reminders
  • 3-move guided set
  • Wellness helper — not medical advice
